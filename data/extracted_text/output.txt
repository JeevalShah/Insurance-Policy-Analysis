DOCUMENT 1
Motor Insurance Claims Compliance Policy
Document ID: MOTOR_CLAIMS_POLICY_001 Version: 2024 Department: Claims Processing
SECTION 1 — Claims Submission
Clause 1.1
Customers must submit vehicle accident claims within 30 calendar days from the date of the accident.
Clause 1.2
All claims must include: 1. FIR copy 2. Vehicle registration certificate 3. Driver license 4. Photographic evidence of damage
Clause 1.3
Claims lacking mandatory documentation shall be marked as incomplete.
Clause 1.4
Incomplete claims shall not proceed to fraud assessment.
SECTION 2 — Fraud Assessment
Clause 2.1
All claims exceeding INR 2,00,000 must undergo fraud risk evaluation.
Clause 2.2
Fraud risk evaluation shall include: 1. Historical claims matching 2. Damage consistency analysis 3. Metadata verification 4. Geo-location consistency checks
Clause 2.3
Claims identified as high-risk must be escalated to manual review.
Clause 2.4
Manual review officers must maintain explainable audit records for every rejected claim.
SECTION 3 — CCTV and Multimedia Validation
Clause 3.1
If CCTV footage is available, it shall be considered supplementary evidence.
Clause 3.2
Dashcam footage may be used for accident chronology reconstruction.
Clause 3.3
Video evidence must not be retained for more than 180 days unless legally mandated.
Clause 3.4
Facial recognition technologies shall not be used during claims investigation.
SECTION 4 — Data Retention
Clause 4.1
Customer claim records must be retained for 7 years.
Clause 4.2
Personally identifiable information shall be encrypted at rest.
Clause 4.3
Audit logs must be immutable.
Clause 4.4
Every automated fraud decision must be explainable and reproducible.
